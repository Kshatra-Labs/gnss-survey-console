GNSS Survey Console

Two u-blox F9P receivers as an RTK pair: centimetre positions, straight from USB in this browser (Chrome or Edge).
  1. Base - receiver that stays put: Connect, Start base, wait for broadcasting.
  2. Rover - receiver you carry: Connect, Live monitor until it shows FIX=true.
  3. Survey origin, then camera_a and camera_b (and any reference points), then open the Site map to check them and write cameras.local.yaml.
