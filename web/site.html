Survey console Site map

Site map - poles, distance and bearing

Uses the points surveyed on the Survey console (same browser), or values you type or read from cameras.local.yaml. Everything is computed here in the page; nothing is uploaded.

Surveyed points

Heights are the receiver's MSL height, as the console records them. "Mount" is how far the camera sits above the surveyed spot (0 if the antenna was held at the camera).

Plan (optional)

The tape-measure layout from the config: poles pole_distance_m apart, camera_b at pole_line_bearing_deg from camera_a, origin halfway between. Fill it in to see how far each pole is from where it should be.

cameras.local.yaml

Paste the file to read its values, or to write the survey into it (origin, each pole's origin_e/n/u, pole_distance_m: 0.0 - the same edits as survey.py point --write). It stays in this tab and is never saved or sent anywhere.
